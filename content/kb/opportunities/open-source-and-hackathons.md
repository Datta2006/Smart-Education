# Open Source & Hackathons

## Objective
Complement your DSA prep with real-world engineering signals — open-source contributions and hackathon projects that interviewers actually read on your resume.

## Opportunities
1. **First open-source PRs** — start with `good first issue` labels on active repos (GSoC-eligible orgs are great targets, see the app's `early-tech-programs` note).
2. **24–48h hackathons** (college, MLH-style, company hackathons) — build something end-to-end in a team; the demo + architecture story is interview gold.
3. **Build a public project repo** — even a small tool with clean README, tests, and CI shows engineering discipline beyond DSA.
4. **Contribute to the SOS-style open tools** — any repo where your PR gets reviewed teaches code review culture early.

## Action Steps
- Aim for one merged open-source PR per semester; treat unmerged PRs as learning, not failure.
- After each hackathon, write a 5-line "what I built, what I'd do differently" note — this becomes interview material.
- Keep it bounded: max ~20% of your weekly prep time so it never cannibalizes DSA.

## Mentor note
DSA gets you the interview; projects get you the offer conversation. A single thoughtful open-source PR with a good commit history often impresses reviewers more than a copied tutorial project — quality and clarity beat volume.
