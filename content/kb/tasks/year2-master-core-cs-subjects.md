# Master Core CS Subjects (DBMS, OS, OOP)

## Objective
By the end of Year 2 you should have a strong, exam-ready grip on the three core subjects that dominate every placement interview: **DBMS, Operating Systems, and OOP**. These subjects have the highest return-on-investment of any coursework you'll take.

## Steps
1. **DBMS:** Learn the full syllabus — relational model, keys, normalization (1NF→BCNF), transactions and ACID, concurrency control, SQL. Use [GeeksforGeeks DBMS](https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/dbms/) and the classic [Database System Concepts slides](https://db-book.com/slides-dir/).
2. **Operating Systems:** Processes vs threads, CPU scheduling, synchronization (semaphores, deadlocks), memory management (paging, segmentation), virtual memory. Study with [StudyTonight OS](https://www.studytonight.com/operating-system/).
3. **OOP:** Encapsulation, abstraction, inheritance, polymorphism — then C++/Java specifics (virtual functions, abstract classes, access specifiers). Practice questions on [InterviewBit OOP](https://www.interviewbit.com/oops-interview-questions/).
4. **Don't just read — write.** For each topic, produce a one-page revision cheat-sheet in your own words. These become your placement revision notes.
5. **Connect to code:** implement a mini DBMS-like query in SQL, write OS scheduling algorithms in C, and build small OOP programs to prove you can apply theory.

## Proof
- A complete set of handwritten/DigitalNotes revision sheets for DBMS, OS, and OOP.
- Solved 20+ practice questions per subject (GFG/interviewbit).
- Score ≥ 80% in your semester exams for these subjects.

## Mentor Note
Companies filter resumes on CGPA first, but they *interview* on core subjects. A student who aces DBMS and OS on the whiteboard clears rounds even with an average CGPA. Year 2 is the last year these subjects feel "easy" — cement them now.
