# Maintain a Structured DSA Problem Log

## Objective
Keep a searchable log of every problem you solve so revision becomes 10x faster and forgetting stops being scary.

## Steps
1. Pick a tool (Notion table, GitHub repo of markdown files, or a spreadsheet) with columns: date, problem, link, topic, pattern, difficulty, time taken, mistakes, complexity.
2. After every problem, spend 3 minutes filling the row — especially the **pattern** and **mistakes** columns; that is where the learning is.
3. Once a week, skim the last 20 rows and re-solve any problem you can't instantly recall the approach for.
4. Before interviews, the log becomes your revision list: filter by pattern or by "stuck" status.

## Proof
- Log has ≥ 50 completed entries with pattern + complexity filled in.
- Weekly review pass is on the calendar.

## Mentor Note
The log is the difference between "I solved 200 problems" and "I can solve 200 problems". A problem you can't re-solve in a minute is one you haven't actually learned. Keep it light — 3 minutes per problem, no essays.
