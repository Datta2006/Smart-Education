# Which DSA Sheet Should You Follow?

## Short Answer
It depends on how much time you have. They overlap heavily — pick one primary sheet and treat the others as topic references.

## Choose Love Babbar 450 if:
- You have 4+ months and want *complete topic coverage* (15 topics, ~450 problems).
- You're targeting Indian companies / on-campus placements where breadth is rewarded.
- You like ticking off a big checklist (the xlsx is in `03.. DSA-SHEETS/`).

## Choose Grokking 14 Patterns if:
- You want *depth per pattern* and interview-style reasoning over raw coverage (~180 curated problems).
- Your goal is product companies / FAANG-style rounds where "explain your approach" is scored.
- You've done some arrays/strings already; the `.mhtml` lessons teach templates.

## Choose Striver A2Z if:
- You're starting from near zero and want a strict step-by-step order (it's also what the SOS app recommends to Year 1 students).
- You like video explanations (takeuforward.org) alongside problems.

## Mentor Recommendation
If you have one strong semester ahead: **Love Babbar 450 as the backbone + Grokking pattern lessons for the hard topics**. Don't maintain three sheets — you'll spend your time updating checklists instead of solving. Whatever you pick, use the problem log (`maintain-dsa-problem-log`) so the sheet actually becomes knowledge.
