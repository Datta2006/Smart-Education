# The Fake Internship Trap

## Mistake
Taking unpaid "internships" at local shops where your job is to photocopy documents or create WhatsApp forwards for "exposure," while your seniors are interning at real tech companies building actual software products.

## Why It Hurts
A fake internship on your resume is a red flag to experienced recruiters. It signals you cannot distinguish between real work and busywork. More importantly, you waste 2-3 months that could have been spent on DSA, building projects, or applying to genuine internship programs.

## Better Action
If you cannot get a real tech internship, use that time to build a portfolio of 2-3 solid projects, contribute to open source, or prepare for internships in Year 2. A strong project portfolio beats a fake internship. Quality over quantity — one genuine internship is worth five fake ones.
