# Which Domain to Specialize In (Web, App, Data, or Core)

## Short Answer
Do NOT lock in permanently in Year 2 — but DO pick a **primary direction** to go deep in, while keeping your fundamentals (DSA + core CS) intact for interviews.

## Choose Web Development (Frontend/Backend/Full-Stack) if:
- You enjoy building products people actually use and seeing results fast.
- You like design, APIs, and end-to-end systems.
- Most software engineering roles (the majority of placements) want web skills. Start with [roadmap.sh/full-stack](https://roadmap.sh/full-stack).

## Choose Data / AI / ML if:
- You're drawn to math, statistics, and problem-solving with data.
- You're targeting data science, ML engineering, or research roles.
- Be careful: AI/ML roles at entry level are fewer than web roles. Keep DSA strong and build a *data portfolio* (see [roadmap.sh/ai-data-scientist](https://roadmap.sh/ai-data-scientist)). Avoid the trap of only watching ML videos (see `ai-ml-fomo-year-1`).

## Choose Core / Systems / DevOps if:
- You love understanding how computers work underneath — OS, networks, compilers, cloud.
- Roles: SRE, DevOps, backend-infra, systems engineering. Fewer but higher-paying. Follow [roadmap.sh/devops](https://roadmap.sh/devops).

## Do Not Decide By:
- What a friend is doing (see `year2-comparison-trap`).
- Salary rumors alone — every domain pays well if you're good.
- "Easiest tutorials" — pick by genuine curiosity.

## Mentor Recommendation
Give each direction a **6-week taste-test** in Year 2: build one tiny project in web, one analysis in data, one system in DevOps. The one where you lose track of time is your signal. Then go deep there in Year 3, but keep DSA and core CS running in parallel — interviews don't care about your domain, they care about fundamentals.
