# Sticking to Easy Problems Only

## Mistake
Farming easy problems for the streak, feeling productive because the numbers go up, and never touching medium/hard problems from the 450 sheet or the Grokking folders.

## Why It Hurts
Easy problems mostly check syntax and one obvious idea. Placement and FAANG interviews are built on medium (and some hard) questions — pattern *combination*. A 100-easy streak teaches you none of the combining skill, and the first medium in an interview will feel like a different sport.

## Better Action
Follow the 70/30 rule: 70% of your daily problems should be at or slightly above your current level (that means medium once basics are done), 30% easy warm-ups. In the 450 sheet and Grokking lessons, never leave a topic with zero mediums solved — the difficulty in each filename is there for a reason.
