# First Hackathon Experience

## Objective
Build something in 24-48 hours under intense time pressure, working in a team with people outside your comfort zone.

## Steps
1. Sign up for your college's first hackathon or a beginner-friendly online hackathon (Devfolio, Unstop).
2. Join a team of 3-4 people — preferably with at least one person skilled in frontend and one in backend.
3. Pick a simple idea (e.g., a study portal, a campus convenience app). Do not over-engineer.
4. Ship a working prototype with basic CRUD functionality and a deployed link.

## Proof
- A GitHub repo with a working prototype and at least 10 commits.
- A deployed link (Vercel/Netlify) and a pitch screenshot.

## Estimated time
30 hours (including the hackathon weekend + a few prep hours).

## Mentor note
Hackathons teach you that shipping a tiny working product under pressure is more valuable than a perfect product that never launches. Most of your teammates will be noisy; learn to filter signal from noise fast.
