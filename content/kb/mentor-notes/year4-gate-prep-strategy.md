# GATE Preparation Strategy & Mindset

## Trigger condition
The student has decided to pursue GATE (PSU/MTech) and needs a structured study plan, or is considering it as a backup and wants to know what it involves.

## Mentor message
GATE CSE is a **single, high-stakes exam** in February — and a marathon that rewards discipline over talent. The good news: most of the syllabus (DBMS, OS, CN, DSA, OOP) is also *interview content* (see `year2-master-core-cs-subjects`), so prep compounds.

**The plan (roughly 8–10 months):**
1. **Know the paper** — ~65 questions in 3 hours, 100 marks, negative marking (−1/3 for MCQs). Weight: Algorithms/DSA, Programming, and core subjects dominate. Solve the previous 10 years' papers first to calibrate.
2. **First pass (months 1–5):** cover subjects from a standard source — [GATEOverflow](https://gateoverflow.in/), [NPTEL](https://nptel.ac.in/), Made Easy/GATE Academy books. Make crisp short notes per subject.
3. **Second pass (months 5–8):** subject-wise pyq (previous year questions) + topic tests. This is where most score is won.
4. **Mock season (months 8–10):** 1–2 full mocks/week, timed, with negative-marking discipline. Analyze every mistake — GATE is as much exam-strategy (time, guessing, order) as knowledge.
5. **Daily routine:** 3–4 focused hours/day (math + core alternating) + weekly revision. Consistency beats 10-hour weekends.

**Mindset:**
- Don't compare your pace to others (see `year2-comparison-trap`).
- If placements are also on your plate, GATE in Feb *plus* placement season is genuinely doable — the prep overlaps (see `year4-job-vs-gate-vs-ms`).
- Your target score: ~600+ for top PSUs, ~700+ for IIT MTech — check the actual cutoffs for the current year.
