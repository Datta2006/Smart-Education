# Feeling Behind in Placement Prep

## Trigger condition
The student sees classmates with higher contest ratings / more solved problems and feels their placement prep is hopelessly behind.

## Mentor message
Comparing your *solved count* to someone else's is comparing the number of steps to the quality of the staircase — and counts are the most gamed metric in DSA. What interviews actually measure is whether you can think in patterns under pressure. A student with 300 logged, re-solved problems routinely outperforms a classmate with 1,000 problems solved once and never revisited. Your assets are the fundamentals (arrays, strings, trees), your problem log with spaced repetition, and the daily consistency routine — all of which compound exactly like an investment. Also remember: ratings and sheet counts are public and loud; mock interviews and rejections are private and quiet, so you only ever see the highlight reel of other people's prep. Run your own race, protect your routine, and audit your actual gap against the 450-sheet topic list rather than against a friend's LeetCode graph.
