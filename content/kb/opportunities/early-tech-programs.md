# Early Tech Programs & Scholarships

## Objective
Apply for programs that give you a competitive edge before Year 2 — research internships, scholarship exams, and elite training programs.

## Opportunities
1. **Google Summer of Code (GSoC)** — Open to all undergraduates. Apply in Year 2, but start contributing to open-source orgs in Year 1.
2. **Microsoft Learn Student Ambassador** — Free training, Azure credits, and networking.
3. **ISCO (IIT Madras) Scholarship Exam** — For students aiming for top-tier higher education.
4. **NPTEL Certification** — Pick 1-2 core subject courses (Python, Data Structures) and earn Elite certificates.
5. **Local University Research Projects** — Approach professors for voluntary research assistant roles.

## Action Steps
- Set a calendar reminder to check application deadlines quarterly.
- Start building a LinkedIn profile that highlights your learning journey.
- Join the "Internship, Placements, higher education" WhatsApp/Telegram groups of your college.

## Mentor note
These programs look at passion and consistency, not perfection. Apply widely, even if you think you are not ready. Rejection is a data point, not a verdict.
