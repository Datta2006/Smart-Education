# Start Competitive Programming & Contests

## Objective
Competitive programming (CP) trains the two things interviews test: **speed** and **handling pressure**. Regular contests in Year 2 make interview coding feel easy by comparison.

## Steps
1. **Pick platforms:** [Codeforces](https://codeforces.com/) (Div 3/Div 4) for algorithm muscle, [CodeChef Starters](https://www.codechef.com/) for Indian-style contests, [LeetCode Weekly](https://leetcode.com/contest/) for interview-format practice.
2. **Solve 2–3 problems daily** using [Neetcode Roadmap](https://neetcode.io/roadmap) to sequence topics. Don't jump around.
3. **Participate weekly:** one contest per week minimum. Even failing teaches you time management.
4. **Upsolve:** after each contest, solve the 1–2 problems you couldn't crack and log the pattern.
5. **Build a streak:** consistency over intensity. Track it in [a simple sheet](https://leetcode.com/) or Notion.

## Proof
- Codeforces rating ≥ 1000 or CodeChef rating ≥ 1400 by end of Year 2.
- A weekly contest participation streak of 10+ weeks.
- 50+ problems solved across arrays, strings, sorting, and searching.

## Mentor Note
CP is not the same as interview prep — interviews test *communication* too. But CP builds the raw problem-solving muscle that makes interviews survivable. Don't chase rating at the cost of your core subjects; 3–4 focused hours a week is enough.
