# Everyone's Placed But Me — The Peer-Pressure Crisis

## Trigger condition
Peers around the student are landing offers while the student isn't yet, and the student feels anxious, inadequate, or under intense pressure to match them.

## Mentor message
When friends start placing, the instinct is to panic-compare. Reframe it:

1. **Placement is a season, not a race to zero.** Companies hire in waves across months. A peer's offer in month 2 doesn't close your window in month 6.
2. **Compare processes, not outcomes.** Ask placed friends *how* they prepared — tests, mocks, questions (see `year3-mock-interview-partners`). Steal their methods, not their timeline.
3. **Your first job is not your final grade.** An offer at company X vs company Y is rarely a life verdict (see `year4-service-vs-product-company`). The only real loss is quitting the process.
4. **Manage the noise.** If campus WhatsApp groups spike your anxiety, mute them and follow your plan. Your preparation sheet is your source of truth.
5. **Structure beats mood.** A daily schedule (aptitude + DSA + mocks + applying) keeps despair at bay — see `year4-aptitude-quant-preparation` and `year4-hr-behavioral-rounds`. Action is the antidote to anxiety.
6. **Remember the "not having backup" trap** — peers who placed may have their own anxieties (bonds, joining windows). You don't see behind their curtain; they don't see yours.

**Concrete step:** ask one placed friend for a mock interview this week. Turn envy into a resource. And see `year4-handling-rejections` for the rejection mindset.
