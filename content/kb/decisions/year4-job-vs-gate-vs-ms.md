# Job vs GATE vs MS — Which Path After B.Tech?

## Short Answer
**Default to Job (placements) first.** It's the highest-probability, lowest-cost path. Keep **GATE as your backup** if placements stall, and pursue **MS only if you have a specific reason** (research career, international work, or your CGPA + finances support it). You can also hedge: give GATE in Feb + place in the spring.

## Choose the Job Track (default) if:
- You want to start earning and building experience now.
- You don't have a burning research/teaching goal.
- Your CGPA is fine but your profile is project/placement-oriented.
- Costs of MS (₹20–60 lakh) or a GATE study year feel risky.

## Choose GATE (PSUs, MTech, higher CS) if:
- You want **PSU jobs** (ONGC, IOCL, NTPC, BHEL, BARC — secure, pensionable) or an **MTech at IIT/NIT** (great for research or deferred career).
- Your placement chances are weak and you want a structured, exam-based path.
- You're willing to study 6–12 months seriously for one exam (see `year4-gate-preparation`). Note: GATE CSE results come in March, right around placement season — you can do both.

## Choose MS Abroad if:
- You want an international career or research track.
- Your CGPA is 8.5+ (US top-50 admissions get easier) and you can afford/fund it.
- You're using MS as a deliberate "second shot" at top companies after graduation.

## Do NOT Decide By:
- FOMO from LinkedIn (see `year2-comparison-trap`).
- One bad placement day — keep options open.
- Ignoring your family's financial situation — MS is a real cost.

## Mentor Recommendation
Run the **triple-track in Year 4**: apply to placements (default), register for GATE in Feb as insurance (the prep helps your core-CS interviews anyway), and only file MS applications if your CGPA and funding check out. The three tracks share ~60% of the preparation (core CS + DSA). Decide the final fork *after* you see placement and GATE outcomes, not before.
