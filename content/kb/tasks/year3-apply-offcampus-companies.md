# Apply to Off-Campus Companies (Targeted, Not Shotgun)

## Objective
Off-campus hiring runs **year-round** and most freshers don't use it effectively. Learn to shortlist companies and apply like a pro — quality over quantity.

## Steps
1. **Shortlist 10–15 companies** from the off-campus fresher list (see `year3-offcampus-fresher-companies`) based on role fit, not just brand. Mix product companies (Amazon, Flipkart, Razorpay), IT services (TCS, Infosys, Wipro), and startups.
2. **Bookmark their career pages** and set alerts — many hire in windows (e.g., TCS NQT, Wipro NLTH, Infosys SP) with predictable cycles.
3. **For each application, customize:** tailor your resume's skills section and your self-intro to the JD (see `year3-build-placement-resume`).
4. **Track in a spreadsheet:** company, role, applied date, status, follow-up. Always follow up after 1–2 weeks.
5. **Use referrals:** ask alumni/seniors (see `year2-find-a-mentor`) for referral links — referred applications are 5–10x more likely to be seen.
6. **Do not apply to all 200 companies** — focus energy on ones you can actually crack (see `career-anti-002`).

## Proof
- 10–15 tailored applications sent with a tracking sheet.
- At least 1 interview or online assessment from the batch.
- A referral obtained from at least 1 alumni contact.

## Mentor Note
Off-campus success = shortlist + prepare + persist. Companies re-open applications quarterly; a rejection now can become an offer in 6 months if you keep improving. Don't treat a "no" as final — treat it as a round you didn't pass *yet*.
