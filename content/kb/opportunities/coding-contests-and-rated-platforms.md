# Coding Contests & Rated Platforms

## Objective
Use weekly rated contests to train speed, pressure-handling, and problem variety — the components no amount of solo practice can simulate.

## Opportunities
1. **LeetCode Weekly + Biweekly Contests** — closest format to coding rounds; use the contest archive for mock sessions.
2. **CodeChef (Starters/ Cook-Off) + Codeforces (Div 3/Div 4)** — great for building the fast-solving muscle and reading tricky problem statements.
3. **GeeksforGeeks weekly coding contest** — common among Indian students pre-placement.
4. **College coding clubs / inter-college hackathons** — low-stakes practice with peer pressure that's actually fun.

## Action Steps
- Put the contest schedule in your calendar (e.g. Codeforces on Saturdays) and treat it as non-negotiable.
- After each contest, upsolve 1–2 problems you couldn't finish and log the pattern you missed.
- Don't chase rating early — chase *participation streak* and upsolving habit for the first 3 months.

## Mentor note
Contests are the only place that simulates interview time pressure. A student who solves mediums comfortably but never competes is always surprised by how different it feels with a clock — train the clock early.
