# The College Placement Reality Check

## Objective
Stop guessing what it takes to get placed in your specific college. Gather hard data from seniors who recently went through the process.

## Steps
1. Identify 2 or 3 final-year students or recent alumni from your college who got placed in good tech companies.
2. Ask them these exact questions:
   - What is the strict CGPA cutoff for top companies visiting our campus?
   - Which specific DSA topics do companies focus on during our campus drives?
   - Are there any specific local companies that hire heavily from our college?
3. Write down their answers in your notes.

## Proof
- A summary note in your Notion/Obsidian/Journal with the exact CGPA cutoff and top 3 skills required for your specific college.

## Estimated time
3 hours (including finding them and having a 20-minute chat).

## Mentor note
Every college has a different placement micro-culture. What works for an IITian might not apply to your campus. Get the ground truth early so you know exactly what CGPA you must protect.
