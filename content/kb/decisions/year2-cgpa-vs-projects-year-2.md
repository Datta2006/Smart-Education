# CGPA vs Projects in Year 2

## Short Answer
**Balance them — and never let projects tank your CGPA below 7.5–8.0.** Year 2 is the year of the *portfolio*, but CGPA remains a hard filter you cannot negotiate later.

## Choose Option A (Protect CGPA ≥ 8.0) if:
- You want on-campus placement eligibility (most companies filter at 7.0–8.0).
- You may pursue MS abroad (universities weigh GPA heavily).
- Your Year 1 CGPA is already below 7.5 — recovering it in Years 2–3 is much harder than protecting it now.

## Choose Option B (Heavy on Projects) if:
- Your CGPA is already ≥ 8.5 and safe — now invest surplus hours in projects.
- You have a startup/income already and placements aren't your goal.
- You're targeting off-campus roles where portfolio > CGPA.

## Do Not Do:
- **Skip exams to finish a project.** One bad semester can drop your CGPA by 0.5–1.0 and eliminate you from shortlists permanently.
- **Build projects as an excuse to avoid tough subjects** (see `year2-skipping-core-subjects`).

## Mentor Recommendation
Allocate 60% of study hours to CGPA (core subjects are also interview topics — see `year2-master-core-cs-subjects`) and 40% to projects + DSA. An 8.0 CGPA with one strong project beats a 6.5 CGPA with five projects, because the 6.5 resume never reaches the human eye.
