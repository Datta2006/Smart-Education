# Practice Top Company Interview Questions

## Objective
Work through the actual question lists saved in `01.. Top Interview Questions Lists/` — the Top 50 general list plus Amazon, Google, Facebook, and LinkedIn PDFs — to learn each company's style.

## Steps
1. Open `0.Top50InterviewQuestions.pdf` first; solve or answer every question, writing the answer before peeking.
2. For your target companies, open their PDF (`1.TopAmazonQuestions.pdf`, `2.TopFacebookQuestions.pdf`, `3.TopGoogleQuestions.pdf`, `4.TopLinkedinQuestions.pdf`).
3. For each question, mark it: solved-cold / solved-with-hint / stuck. Revisit the "stuck" set within a week.
4. Look for repeated topics per company (e.g. Amazon loves arrays & greedy; Google leans harder on graphs and DP) and over-train those.
5. In the final week, do the top 50 as a timed written test.

## Proof
- Every question in the Top 50 and your target company list is answered and logged.
- You can state 3 repeated topics per target company.

## Mentor Note
Company lists reveal *frequency*, not absolutes — a question asked by Amazon twice is more likely to recur than a random LeetCode hard. But never skip the core (arrays, strings, trees) chasing company quirks; fundamentals are what every list is built on.
