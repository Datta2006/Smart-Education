# No Backup Plan (All Eggs in One Basket)

## Mistake
Betting everything on a single path — "I'll only go for Amazon, nothing else", "I'm 100% MS abroad", or "placements will work out" — with zero plan B if it fails.

## Why It Hurts
Placements are competitive and seasonal; a single-company strategy can leave you with nothing at the end of the season. MS applications can be rejected or unfunded. GATE requires 6+ months of serious prep. Students who tie their entire future to one outcome spend the last semester in panic when it doesn't land — and some miss every other window.

## Better Action
**Run parallel tracks** (see `year4-job-vs-gate-vs-ms`): placements as the default, GATE in February as insurance, MS applications only if they fit your profile. Also apply to *multiple* companies within each track (see `year3-apply-offcampus-companies`). Hedging isn't lack of commitment — it's what successful applicants actually do. A backup plan means you're never negotiating from fear.
