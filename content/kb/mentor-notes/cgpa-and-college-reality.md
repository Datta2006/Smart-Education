# CGPA and College Reality Check

## Trigger condition
Student panics about placements, asking "Will I get a job?" or "Is my CGPA too low?" early in their degree.

## Mentor message
The single biggest factor that determines whether you get shortlisted by companies is your **CGPA**. At most state-private and even tier-2 engineering colleges, a CGPA cutoff of 8.0 or 8.5 is a hard gate — if you are below it, you will not even get an interview, regardless of how many projects you have. Year 1 subjects are the easiest you will ever face. A 9.0 CGPA in Year 1 is very achievable if you attend classes and study 1 hour before exams. Protect your CGPA like a bodyguard; you will thank yourself in Year 3.
