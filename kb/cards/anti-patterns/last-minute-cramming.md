# Last-Minute Interview Cramming

## Mistake
Starting real DSA practice 3–4 weeks before interviews, solving 10 problems a day in panic mode, and hoping the pattern recognition magically appears on interview day.

## Why It Hurts
DSA is a recognition and speed skill built over months of daily reps. Cramming produces shallow familiarity with *specific* problems, no depth, and high interview anxiety. Companies also notice the gap when the same candidate blanks on fundamentals mid-round.

## Better Action
Start at least 4–6 months early with the daily consistency routine (see `daily-dsa-consistency-routine`). Use the 6-Week crash course (`six-week-interview-crash-course`) only as the *final polish* over an existing base. If you truly are at the last month, prioritize patterns + medium problems over breadth, and do mocks daily — don't add new topics in the final week.
