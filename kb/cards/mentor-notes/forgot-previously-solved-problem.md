# Forgetting Problems You Already Solved

## Trigger condition
The student re-encountered a problem they solved weeks ago and couldn't recall the approach, and is doubting their progress.

## Mentor message
Forgetting is the default state of the human brain — it is not a sign of low talent. The graph of DSA skill is not "remember everything forever"; it's a decay curve that only becomes flat with *spaced repetition*. That's exactly why the problem log (`maintain-dsa-problem-log`) exists: once a week, re-solve the problems you can no longer instantly classify. If you can re-derive the pattern in a minute, the knowledge survived; if not, that week's review caught it before interview day would have. Expect to re-solve 30–40% of your early problems. Professionals re-solve old problems constantly — the recall itself is the skill you're training, not the first solve.
