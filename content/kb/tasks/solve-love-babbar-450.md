# Solve the Love Babbar 450 DSA Sheet

## Objective
Work through the 450-problem DSA sheet (available in `Interview-Preparation-Notes-master/03.. DSA-SHEETS/`) to build complete coverage of the topics asked in placement interviews.

## Steps
1. Download `Love Babbar 450 DSA Sheet.xlsx` from the notes folder and convert it to a tracking checklist (Notion/Excel/Google Sheets).
2. Attack topics in this order: Arrays → Matrix → Strings → Searching & Sorting → Linked List → Stack/Queue → Trees → Graph → DP.
3. Solve 2–3 problems per day from the current topic; do not skip the "hard" ones — mark them and return within a week.
4. For every problem, record the pattern used and the time/space complexity in your problem log.
5. After finishing a topic, re-solve 5 random problems from it as a spaced-repetition pass.

## Proof
- The 450 checklist shows every topic ≥ 80% solved, with no topic left at 0%.
- Your problem log has an entry with pattern + complexity for each solved problem.

## Mentor Note
The 450 sheet is a coverage map, not a race. Depth on a hard graph problem beats speed through 10 easy ones. Consistency is what separates students who clear interviews from those who collect sheets.
