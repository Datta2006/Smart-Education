# Imposter Syndrome in Year 2

## Trigger condition
The student is doing the work (projects, DSA, contests) but still feels like a fraud — convinced that everyone else is more "real" than them, and that they'll be "found out".

## Mentor message
Imposter syndrome is a *calibration* problem, not a competence problem. You now know enough to see how much exists that you don't know — that's what progress feels like from the inside. The classmate who looks unbeatable is comparing their highlights to your full reel. Facts over feelings: count your shipped project, your solved problems, your contest streak. That's your real level. You don't need to be the best — you need to be *better than you were in semester 1*, and by that measure you're already winning. Keep going; the feeling fades as your proof compounds.
