# Jumping to AI/ML Without Fundamentals

## Mistake
Watching a 2-hour "Build a Neural Network in Python" YouTube video and claiming you know Machine Learning, while still struggling to write a loop that iterates through an array.

## Why It Hurts
AI/ML is applied mathematics and advanced logic. If you cannot manipulate basic data structures, you are just copy-pasting code you do not understand. In interviews, you will be asked about the math or the underlying logic, and you will fail immediately.

## Better Action
Master Python syntax, data structures, and basic statistics first. Wait until Year 2 or Year 3 to build AI projects when you actually understand the code you are writing.
