# Master Touch Typing & Dev Shortcuts

## Objective
Stop hunting and pecking at the keyboard. You will write code for the rest of your life; a slow typing speed is a permanent tax on your time.

## Steps
1. Go to sites like 10FastFingers or TypingTest.
2. Practice touch typing for 15 minutes daily until you consistently hit 35-40 WPM. You don't need 100 WPM, but you must type without looking at the keys.
3. Learn essential OS shortcuts. Stop using the mouse for everything. 
4. Master `Ctrl + Backspace` (deletes a whole word instead of one letter) and `Ctrl + Shift + Arrow` (highlights text instantly).

## Proof
- A screenshot of a 40+ WPM test result.
- A conscious effort to use only the keyboard when navigating your IDE.

## Mentor Note
No interviewer will ask your WPM. But if you are typing at 15 WPM during a 45-minute coding interview, you will run out of time to solve the actual logic problem.
