# PSU Jobs Through GATE

## Objective
Several Indian Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs) recruit Computer Science engineers **through GATE scores** — no separate entrance exam, just a GATE score + a short interview. These are highly secure, well-paid, pensionable government jobs.

## Opportunities (via GATE score + interview)
- **ONGC** — Computer Science engineer roles, attractive pay packages.
- **IOCL** (Indian Oil) — Engineering/Computer roles.
- **NTPC** — Software/IT engineer roles.
- **BHEL** — Computer/IT engineering roles.
- **SAIL, NALCO, NLC** — occasional CS recruitments.
- **BARC** (Department of Atomic Energy) — through GATE + separate interview, highly prestigious.
- **NIELIT, PGCIL, PowerGrid, AAI** — periodic GATE-based recruitments.

## How It Works
1. Register for **GATE** (usually Sept, exam in Feb) → https://gate.iitb.ac.in/
2. Score well (top PSUs typically need ~600–800 depending on the year).
3. Apply to each PSU's recruitment drive (they publish separate notifications using the GATE score).
4. Clear the (usually easy) interview → get the PSU offer.

## Key Points
- **Age limit:** 18–27 years (relaxation for reserved categories). Apply in Year 4 while you're within age.
- **Bond/security:** some PSUs require a bond; understand terms before joining.
- **Pay:** PSU pay is competitive with private sector at entry, with far more job security, and often better work-life balance.

## Mentor Note
PSUs are the quiet gems of the GATE route — government job security with engineer-level pay. If you're a GATE aspirant (see `year4-gate-prep-strategy`), treat PSU recruitment as a *second* use of the same score. Even if you prefer a private role later, having a PSU offer as a fallback changes your negotiation power completely.
