# CGPA vs Projects in Year 1

## Short Answer
Prioritize CGPA in Year 1. Aim for 8.0+. Do not tank your grades to build beginner-level projects.

## Choose Option A (Focus on CGPA > 8.0) if:
- You want to sit for top-tier off-campus and on-campus internships in Year 3.
- You are considering an MS abroad (universities look at overall GPA).
- You are undecided on your exact career path (a high CGPA keeps all doors open).

## Choose Option B (Focus heavily on Projects, ignore CGPA) if:
- You already have a working startup or a serious freelance business that generates income.
- You are building a highly complex, open-source tool that has real users (not just a to-do list app).

## Do Not Choose Either If:
- You are using "building projects" as an excuse because you are lazy to study for your Physics or Engineering Mechanics exams.

## Mentor Recommendation
Year 1 subjects are relatively easy and non-core. It takes minimal effort to score an 8.5 CGPA if you study consistently. Many companies have a strict 8.0 or 8.5 cutoff for internship shortlists. If your CGPA is 6.5, the recruiter will never even see your amazing Year 1 projects. Protect the GPA now; grind projects in Year 2.
