# Prepare Your Self-Introduction (The 2-Minute Pitch)

## Objective
"Tell me about yourself" opens **almost every interview round** — technical and HR. A practiced, structured self-intro sets a confident tone and lets you steer the conversation toward your strengths.

## Steps
1. **Structure it (2–5 minutes max):**
   - Name, department, college, latest CGPA.
   - Top 3 strengths (pitch yourself as a solid hire).
   - Short-term and long-term goals (optional).
   - Top skills + tech stack, tailored to the role.
   - Top achievements: hackathons, contests, publications (proof of tech + soft skills).
   - Positions of responsibility (leadership, teamwork).
   - Briefly: top projects and hobbies (only if time permits).
2. **Put highlights first.** Interviewers may interrupt mid-way — say achievements and skills in the first minutes.
3. **Practice out loud daily** in front of a mirror (or record yourself). Speak with clarity and volume, keep eye contact, sit straight.
4. **Tailor it per company/role** — tweak the skills section to match the JD.

## Proof
- A written 2-minute script you can deliver without reading.
- Recorded practice videos — review 3 of them for filler words ("um", "like").
- Delivered it successfully in at least one mock interview.

## Mentor Note
Your self-intro is a **pitch, not a bio**. Every line should make the interviewer think "this person can do the job." Confidence is built by repetition — 20 recorded rehearsals will make you sound effortless in the real thing.
