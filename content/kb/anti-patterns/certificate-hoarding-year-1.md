# The Certificate Hoarding Trap

## Mistake
Enrolling in 5 online courses simultaneously, collecting certificates like badges, and posting "NPTEL + Coursera + Udemy" on your resume, while your LeetCode profile shows zero problems solved.

## Why It Hurts
Certificates are not skills. Companies do not hire you because you have 20 online course certificates; they hire you because you can solve problems and build things. Recruititer eyes glaze over when they see a wall of certificates with no demonstrable coding ability.

## Better Action
Finish one course completely, build a small project with it, and push it to GitHub. One real project beats five certificates every time. Focus on depth over breadth.
