# Which Tech Domain to Explore in Year 2

## Short Answer
Based on your Year 1 experience, choose one primary domain to double down on in Year 2. You cannot master everything at once.

## Choose Software Engineering (Full-Stack / Backend) if:
- You enjoyed solving DSA problems and building CLI tools.
- You are strong in C++, Java, or Python and want to crack coding interviews.
- You want to join product companies (FAANG, startups) or prepare for placements.

## Choose Data Science / AI if:
- You genuinely enjoy math, statistics, and number-crunching.
- You scored well in Mathematics and Probability courses.
- You are considering an MS abroad in CS or Data Science.

## Choose Research / MS Abroad if:
- You have a strong academic record (9.0+ CGPA) and good faculty relationships.
- You are genuinely curious about theoretical computer science or research.
- You can afford (or plan to fund) higher education abroad.

## Mentor Recommendation
If you are unsure, default to **Software Engineering + Placements**. It is the safest, highest-paying path for most students. The DSA and CP skills you build also transfer to any domain later.
