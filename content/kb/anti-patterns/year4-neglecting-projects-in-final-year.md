# Neglecting Projects in the Final Year

## Mistake
Treating the final year as "only placement tests + GATE + MS apps" and letting your FYP and projects rot. Or, the reverse — doing everything except finishing your FYP, so your major project becomes a last-month scramble.

## Why It Hurts
Your FYP is the **most interviewable item** on your Year 4 resume — both companies and MS committees ask about it in detail (see `year4-final-year-project`). A rushed, half-finished FYP reads as "cannot deliver" no matter how many tests you pass. Conversely, students who nail a working FYP + demo consistently convert more offers, because the project proves real engineering skill that aptitude and DSA alone don't.

## Better Action
- **Plan your FYP from month 1** with milestones, and protect 4–6 hours a week for it throughout the year (see `year4-final-year-project`).
- Keep your **project pipeline warm**: even small personal projects or open-source PRs (see `year2-gsoc-and-open-source`) keep your portfolio fresh.
- Don't let interview prep *replace* project work — interviews *are* partly about projects. Budget both: 60% interview prep, 40% project work is a reasonable split.
