# The Comparison Trap (Peers, Seniors, and Social Media)

## Mistake
Measuring your progress against a friend who "already has an internship", a senior with a 2000 Codeforces rating, or a LinkedIn post showing someone's 10th hackathon win — and then feeling worthless or rushing your plan.

## Why It Hurts
Comparison is a *distraction from your own roadmap*. When you compare, you either get discouraged (and stop) or you panic-switch strategies (and lose momentum). Social media only shows curated highlights, never the failures behind them. The student who appears ahead at Year 2 is often burnt out by Year 3 — the race is a marathon.

## Better Action
Compare against **yourself last week**. Track your own progress: problems solved, projects shipped, contests participated. Every student moves at a different pace because they start from a different baseline. If someone is genuinely ahead, learn *how* they work — not envy their results.
