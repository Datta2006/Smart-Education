# Revise Core CS Concepts (OOPs, OS, DBMS)

## Objective
Revise the theory subjects every CS interview asks about — Object-Oriented Programming, Operating Systems, and Database Management Systems — using the consolidated notes in `Interview-Preparation-Notes-master/README.md`.

## Steps
1. OOPs: revise class/object, the 4 pillars (encapsulation, abstraction, inheritance, polymorphism), constructors, `this`, friend functions, virtual & pure virtual functions, access specifiers.
2. OS: revise process vs thread, scheduling algorithms (FCFS, SJF, SRTF, RR, Priority, HRRN, MLQ, MLFQ), critical section + mutex/semaphore, deadlock conditions & handling, paging vs segmentation, page replacement (FIFO, Optimal, LRU), disk scheduling (FCFS, SSTF, SCAN, C-SCAN, LOOK, C-LOOK).
3. DBMS: revise ER model, keys (super, candidate, primary, foreign), functional dependencies, normalization (1NF→BCNF), transactions & states, ACID, serializability, recoverable schedules, relational algebra operators.
4. Make a one-page cheat sheet per subject and do a weekly self-quiz.

## Proof
- One-page cheat sheet per subject, reviewed twice.
- Can answer a random question from each subject out loud in under 2 minutes.

## Mentor Note
These are the "warm-up" questions interviewers ask before DSA. You don't need depth — you need crisp definitions and examples. The README notes are dense and exam-style; convert them into your own words or they will not survive interview pressure.
