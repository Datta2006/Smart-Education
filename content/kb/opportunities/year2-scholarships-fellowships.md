# Scholarships & Fellowships for CS Students

## Objective
There are more scholarships and fellowships for CS students than most people know. Many go unclaimed every year simply because nobody applies. Year 2 is the right time to start a scholarship radar.

## Opportunities
1. **Google Summer of Code (stipend)** — technically a stipend program, but it's the classic "paid open-source" starting point for CS students. → https://summerofcode.withgoogle.com/
2. **Google Generation Scholarship** — For women studying computer science/related fields in India; financial award + retreat. → https://buildyourfuture.withgoogle.com/scholarships/generation-google-scholarship/
3. **Microsoft Learn Student Ambassadors** — Free program (now open to all disciplines): training, Azure credits, community leadership — a strong resume + networking line. → https://mvp.microsoft.com/en-US/studentambassadors
4. **NPTEL** — Free IIT-certified courses (Elite + Silver/Gold certificates). Great for core subjects and boosts your profile cheaply. → https://nptel.ac.in/
5. **Outreachy** — Paid remote internships for under-represented groups. → https://www.outreachy.org/
6. **CRA DREU** (US-focused) — Research experiences for undergraduates. → https://cra.org/cra-wp/dreu/
7. **College-level scholarships** — Check your institution's cell for merit/need-based CS scholarships every semester; most are applied for once and renewable.

## Action Steps
- Set quarterly calendar reminders to check the above application windows.
- Keep an "opportunities" doc that tracks deadlines, eligibility, and application status.
- Apply even if you think you're not ready — rejection is a data point, not a verdict.

## Mentor note
Scholarships look at **passion and consistency**, not perfection. The act of applying — writing your story, gathering your work — is itself good portfolio practice. And a Microsoft or Google line on your resume signals quality to every recruiter that follows.
