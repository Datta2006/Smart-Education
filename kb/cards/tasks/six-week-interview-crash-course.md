# Follow the 6-Week Interview Crash Course

## Objective
Execute the structured `07.. [6 Weeks to Interview Ready]` module (6 modules + bonuses in the notes folder) to compress your final revision into a six-week sprint before interview season.

## Steps
1. Open `07.. [6 Weeks to Interview Ready]/` and inspect `Module 0` (Resource list) — it tells you what to gather before starting.
2. Follow modules 1→6 in order, spending roughly one week per module: foundation revision, DSA sprint, coding contest practice, project + resume polish, mock interviews, and final review.
3. Use the `Bonuses` folder for extras (resume templates, cheat sheets).
4. Track daily progress against the module checklists; if you fall behind, protect the mock-interview week — never skip it.

## Proof
- Every module checklist completed with dates.
- At least 3 full mock interviews (with another person or recorded) completed in week 5–6.

## Mentor Note
This course works because it forces *testing* yourself (contests, mocks) rather than just reading. The last two weeks should feel like exam revision: same hours, same sleep, low novelty. Trust the plan; don't start new topics in week 6.
