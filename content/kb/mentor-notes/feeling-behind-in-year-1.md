# Feeling Behind Compared to "Coder" Peers

## Trigger condition
Student feels discouraged because some classmates already know web development, use Linux, or talk about advanced frameworks.

## Mentor message
It is completely normal to feel behind in the first year. Some of your peers have been coding since 9th grade. But engineering is a 4-year marathon, not a sprint. Many "advanced" freshmen burn out by Year 3 because they never learned how to study or manage their time. Focus on your own baseline. Master your logic, keep your grades solid, and stay consistent. You will catch up and surpass them if you just don't quit.
