# Survive Club Recruitment (Learn OOPs)

## Objective
Technical clubs in your college will start recruiting. They will ask Object-Oriented Programming (OOP) questions in their technical interviews. Learn enough to pass.

## Steps
1. Learn the 4 pillars: Encapsulation, Abstraction, Inheritance, Polymorphism.
2. Understand Classes, Objects, Constructors, and Access Modifiers in your chosen language (C++/Java).
3. Build a mini CLI project using OOP (e.g., a Library Management System or a Bank Account simulator).
4. Apply to 2 technical clubs in your college. 

## Proof
- A GitHub repo containing your OOP-based mini project.
- Clearing the technical round of at least one college tech club.

## Mentor Note
Joining a tech club is the fastest way to meet seniors who have already cracked placements. The peer pressure and guidance you get here are worth more than any online course.
