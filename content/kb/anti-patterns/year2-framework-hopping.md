# Framework Hopping (Shiny Object Syndrome)

## Mistake
Starting with React in February, switching to Django in April "because backend is better", trying Flutter in June, and returning to React in September — all without finishing a single project.

## Why It Hurts
Every framework switch resets your momentum. You end up knowing the *names* of five frameworks and the *basics* of none. Recruiters don't care how many frameworks you've touched — they care about depth in one. A student who built one polished app in one stack beats a student who started six apps in six stacks.

## Better Action
Pick **one** stack for the year (e.g., MERN or Django or Next.js+Postgres) and finish at least one deployed project in it. Learn additional frameworks only when a specific project demands it. Depth is the differentiator.
