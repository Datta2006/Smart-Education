# Your First Internship Application (Off-Campus)

## Objective
Year 2 is when you learn the *process* of applying for internships — resume, cover letter, cold outreach — so that Year 3's internship season is a formality, not a panic.

## Steps
1. **Write a one-page resume** using a clean template ([FlowCV](https://flowcv.com/) or [Overleaf](https://www.overleaf.com/)). Keep it ATS-friendly: single column, no photos, simple fonts.
2. **Run it through a scorer** like [Resume Worded](https://resumeworded.com/score) — a low ATS score gets you filtered before any human reads it.
3. **Target the right companies:** startups and off-campus programs hire Year 2 students more than big firms. Check [Internshala](https://internshala.com/), [LinkedIn jobs](https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/), and [Wellfound (AngelList)](https://wellfound.com/).
4. **Cold-email 10–20 founders/engineers** at startups with a short, specific note about their product + how you can help. This works far better than blind applications for Year 2 students.
5. **Track everything** in a spreadsheet — company, stage, date, follow-up. Never let a follow-up slip.
6. **Attend college placement-cell and alumni sessions** to learn what on-campus recruiters filter on.

## Proof
- A polished one-page resume that scores ≥ 80 on an ATS checker.
- 10+ applications or cold emails sent, with a tracking sheet.
- At least 1 interview or meaningful recruiter response (this is a process-win, even without an offer).

## Mentor Note
In Year 2, an internship is a *bonus*, not a requirement — the real goal is learning how the game works. Rejection is data: if you get a "no", ask what you can improve. By Year 3, this pipeline should be second nature.
