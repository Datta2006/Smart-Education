# Build a Placement-Ready Resume

## Objective
Create a **one-page, ATS-scannable** resume that gets you past automated filters and tells a recruiter why you're hireable in the ~30 seconds they actually spend reading it.

## Steps
1. **One page only.** Recruiters skim; details on page 2 get missed.
2. **Sections in order:** contact info → skills → education → experience/projects → achievements/certifications. (Profile line optional on top; positions of responsibility + hobbies at the bottom.)
3. **Contact block:** name, phone, professional email, LinkedIn, GitHub, and coding profiles (LeetCode/CodeChef) if you have a good rating.
4. **Skills on top, sub-sectioned:** languages (C, C++, Python…), databases (MySQL, PostgreSQL…), frameworks (React, Django…), tools (Git, Docker…), areas (DSA, Web Dev, ML).
5. **Projects & internships:** crisp 2–3 lines — *what problem, what you built, quantitative impact* — then the tech stack on its own line. No vague fluff.
6. **PDF, single column, simple font.** Avoid columns, colors, pictures, and graphs that break ATS parsers.
7. **Run it through a scorer** (Resume Worded, Enhancv) and fix a low score before applying anywhere.
8. **Proofread twice** — no typos, only your top 3–4 projects/achievements with working, public links.

## Proof
- Fits on one page and scores high on an ATS checker.
- Every link on it is publicly viewable and works.
- A friend can find your top 3 strengths in 30 seconds of reading it.

## Mentor Note
Your resume is a **marketing document**, not an autobiography. Add what survives the 30-second scan: numbers, tech stacks, and outcomes. Adjectives ("passionate", "hardworking") convince no one — metrics do.
