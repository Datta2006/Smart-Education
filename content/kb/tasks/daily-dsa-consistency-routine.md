# Build a Daily DSA Consistency Routine

## Objective
Establish a fixed daily DSA habit — small enough to never skip — so that interview prep compounds instead of cramming.

## Steps
1. Fix one non-negotiable slot (e.g. 7:00–8:00 AM) for DSA every day, including weekends.
2. Structure the hour: 10 min review of yesterday's problem, 40 min on one new problem (20 min struggle → 20 min study the solution if stuck), 10 min log entry.
3. Never miss two days in a row — one missed day is a slip, two is a habit break.
4. Track a streak, but define success as *showing up*, not problem count.

## Proof
- 30-day streak tracker showing daily DSA sessions.
- Problem log grows by ~1 entry/day.

## Mentor Note
An hour a day for a year beats a 12-hour weekend every time. Motivation is unreliable; routines are not. On your worst day, solve one easy problem — the point is to keep the chain alive, and most days you'll end up doing more anyway.
