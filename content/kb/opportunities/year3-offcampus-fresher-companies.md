# Off-Campus Companies That Hire Freshers Year-Round

## Objective
On-campus drives are limited to companies that visit your college. Off-campus hiring is **24/7 and global** — and freshers who know where to apply get far more shots. This card is your shortlist.

## Categories & Companies

### 1. IT Services / Mass Recruiters (regular windows, easiest entry)
- **TCS** — TCS NQT (National Qualifier Test) runs multiple times a year → https://www.tcs.com/careers
- **Infosys** — Infosys SP/System Engineer hiring → https://www.infosys.com/careers/
- **Wipro** — Wipro NLTH (National Level Talent Hunt) → https://careers.wipro.com/
- **Cognizant** — GenC program → https://careers.cognizant.com/
- **Capgemini** — Exceller program → https://www.capgemini.com/careers/
- **Tech Mahindra** → https://careers.techmahindra.com/
- **HCL** → https://www.hcltech.com/careers
- **Accenture** → https://www.accenture.com/in-en/careers
- **IBM** — entry-level software roles → https://www.ibm.com/careers

### 2. Product Companies (more selective, better pay)
- **Amazon** → https://www.amazon.jobs/
- **Flipkart** → https://www.flipkartcareers.com/
- **Razorpay** → https://razorpay.com/jobs/
- **Zomato, Swiggy** → careers pages open frequently for SDE-1
- **PhonePe, Paytm, CRED** — Bangalore fintech, high bar
- **Atlassian, Intuit, Salesforce India** — global product cos with India offices

### 3. Startups (learn fast, easier entry, intern-to-offer)
- Wellfound (AngelList) startup jobs → https://wellfound.com/
- Internshala → https://internshala.com/
- LinkedIn jobs → https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/

### 4. PSU / Government (GATE route, separate track)
- ONGC, IOCL, NTPC, BHEL, BARC, ISRO, NIELIT, SAIL, NALCO — through **GATE** scores (see `year3-gate-ms-decision`).

## Action Steps
- Bookmark the career pages above and check weekly (they post windows + deadlines).
- Register for TCS NQT, Wipro NLTH, Infosys SP early — spots and windows fill.
- Apply to product companies via referrals (see `year2-find-a-mentor`).
- Keep a tracking sheet: company, role, deadline, status.

## Mentor note
Cast a **wide net but a targeted lure**: mass recruiters get you an offer and a start; product companies get you faster growth. Both are valid — do the ones you can realistically crack first, then climb. Off-campus is a numbers + persistence game, so start early in Year 3.
